# Dashboard surface

Mode: Operate. Route: /.
Approved direction: Atlas, selected in `.impeccable/mocks/decision/atlas.webp`.

The full dashboard uses a paper canvas, navy ink, cobalt controls, green offer-path and red rejection accents, DM Serif Display headlines and numbers, Manrope evidence and controls, and Georgia desktop navigation and tagline. The fixed left index is 240px wide, narrowing to 190px at 1250px; at 950px it becomes top navigation.

Opening sequence: editorial masthead and explicit live/demo provenance, compact primary filters, then the current-status route with enlarged count circles and inline cohort shares. Date bounds, source, and tier sit in a disclosure. Supporting evidence follows in ruled spreads: activity beside submission metrics, geography, distributions, follow-ups, the synthetic Explorer ledger, and methodology.

Route: Total tracked → Discovered → Preparing → Applied → Recruiter Screen → Interview → Technical → Final → Offer. Rejected branches separately; Unknown remains separate. Total includes pre-submission opportunities and submission conflicts. Counts and cohort shares describe current status, never historical transitions or conversion rates.

Stations are read-only, with no detail panel or scroll action. Offer is not an acceptance claim. Country selection filters the aggregate cohort. Explorer search, filters, sorting, and pagination operate independently on reviewed fictional records.

Responsive behavior: the route becomes vertical at 650px, and evidence spreads stack. Wide tables scroll locally; the Explorer has a keyboard-focusable scroll region and a visible scroll hint at narrower widths. Two short ring pulses and a connector reveal finish within four seconds; reduced motion keeps nodes and lines still.

Keep snapshot, denominator, empty-state, and provenance copy. Never infer rejections, invent trends, expose raw private records, or hide synthetic labeling. DESIGN.md and its schema-v2 sidecar capture the shared visual system.
