# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Bun, React, strict TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind, Plotly/react-plotly.js, Cloudflare Workers, Google Sheets, Vitest. Explicit user choices.

## Users

Public visitors evaluating a Data Science / Product Analytics portfolio; the owner maintaining a personal job application tracker.

## Product Purpose

Turn the tracker into a technically credible, engaging public analytics dashboard with minimal maintenance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

One Worker deployment, private Sheets source, aggregated live analytics, an Explorer of approved live application fields, consistent filters and definitions. No fabricated recruitment history, response rates, or conversion funnels. Only approved company, role, country, source, tier, status and application date fields may enter the Explorer; all other source fields stay private. Approved design and plan in docs/superpowers/.

## Brand Commitments

Peak Unemployment. “Because apparently even unemployment needs KPIs.” Professional, slightly playful, neutral and restrained; independent of the owner's portfolio design.

## Evidence on Hand

Only synthetic fixtures are committed. During the Atlas redesign, the browser observed a locally configured live aggregate response. Credentials were not inspected; underlying private-row accuracy and production deployment were not audited. No raw private records were exposed through the observed dashboard.

## Product Principles

Analytical accuracy; privacy by construction; understandable visualizations; small maintainable implementation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive desktop/mobile, keyboard controls, readable labels, tabular chart alternatives, loading/empty/error states.
